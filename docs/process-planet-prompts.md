# PNG de planetas para Cómo trabajo

Generados con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, con `transparent_background: true`. Cada PNG corresponde a una generación independiente. La integración usa imágenes decorativas con texto alternativo vacío y reserva sus dimensiones para evitar saltos de layout.

## Prompt final (sustituir SUBJECT con cada descripción de la lista)

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated small planet icon: SUBJECT. Transparent background PNG, square composition. High-quality tactile miniature 3D painted space adventure style, warm handmade planetary art inspired by Outer Wilds emotional atmosphere, entirely original. Detailed natural surface texture and dimensional relief, not a smooth gradient ball and not a flat vector icon. Full spherical planet centered with generous 12% transparent padding, light from upper left, lower right shaded but still showing rock detail, realistic small miniature proportions. Must remain readable when displayed at 64px on cream paper background. No rings, no orbit lines, no stars, no scene, no labels, no text, no extra objects, no backdrop, no ground plane, no cast shadow outside the sphere. Keep clean anti-aliased silhouette and alpha transparency. Match a cohesive set of four portfolio process illustrations.
